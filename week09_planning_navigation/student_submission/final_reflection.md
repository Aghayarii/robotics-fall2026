## Response

This activity helped me understand how algorithms we learn in computer science are actually used in robotics. I found it interesting that two algorithms can find the same shortest path but still have different performance when the robot follows it. What stood out to me the most was the human-aware navigation part. Before this lab, I mostly thought successful navigation meant avoiding obstacles and reaching the goal. Now I understand that robots also need to consider the people around them and how their movements might affect others.
This activity made me more interested in the connection between artificial intelligence and robotics. I also realized that improving an algorithm is not always about making it faster. Sometimes making it safer and more appropriate for real-world situations is just as important.
