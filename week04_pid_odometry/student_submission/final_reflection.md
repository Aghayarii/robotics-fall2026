This activity helped me understand how PID control and odometry work together in a robot. What stood out to me was that even when a controller is tuned well, the robot can still follow the wrong path if its position estimate is inaccurate. Adjusting the PID gains showed me how small changes can affect overshoot, oscillation, and how smoothly the robot reaches a target. I also saw how small odometry errors can build up over time and cause the estimated path to drift from the real path.

I also liked the part about pedestrian safety because it showed that tuning is not only about making a robot faster or more accurate. Engineers also have to think about how the robot behaves around people and leave enough safety margin even if that means reducing speed. Overall, this activity made the connection between control, sensing, and safety much clearer to me.
